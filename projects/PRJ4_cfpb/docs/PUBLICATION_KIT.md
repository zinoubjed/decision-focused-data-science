## Notion case-study copy



### A confident suggestion can still be wrong for the people in a rare category.



**Decision.** Assist intake staff with consumer-product suggestions, abstention and human review. Product selection is a proxy, not institutional routing ground truth.



**Evidence.** Selected logistic_tfidf on validation macro-F1. Novel temporal test n=4,677: accuracy 80.74%, macro-F1 0.632, majority accuracy 54.12%. Threshold 0.6 has 273 wrong accepted suggestions; 1,506 low-confidence novel cases require review. Overall sampled workflow: 3,171/8,218 suggestions accepted (38.59%); 5,047 assigned to review including withheld text repeats. The subgroup review reinforces the recommendation: human verification is required; aggregate accepted accuracy is not a per-class routing guarantee.



**Interpretation.** No automatic routing recommended: aggregate accepted accuracy hides weak true-class outcomes. Results are on a 3% ID sample and template-filtered cohort. Character matching is not semantic deduplication. Confidence/Wilson search does not give a post-selection probability guarantee. Private text and vocabulary excluded; human-verification workflow required.



**Handoff.** Review the source, tests and population boundaries before using this as a business result. This is a portfolio study and local technical demonstration, not a client deployment. Personal autonomous proficiency remains unverified. Source and rights: CFPB official Historical Narratives Archive; public reuse statement, no blanket CC assertion.
