## Notion case-study copy



### A delivery KPI is only useful when its denominator survives the joins.



**Decision.** Operations analyst prioritises delivery segments for investigation after outcomes mature. No purchase-time prediction or causal sanction.



**Evidence.** SQL retained 99,441 unique orders. All-period late rate is 6,534/96,470 = 6.77%, conditional on eligible delivered orders. Mature 60-day cohort rate: 6.85%.



**Interpretation.** Noncommercial Olist restriction. Undelivered orders are outside the late-rate denominator and explicitly counted. Export date is not known; latest purchase is a proxy, with 30/60/90-day maturity sensitivities. Wilson intervals assume independent Bernoulli outcomes; repeat-customer/route dependence and confounding remain.
