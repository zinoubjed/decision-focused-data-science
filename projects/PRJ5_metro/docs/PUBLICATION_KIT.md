## Notion case-study copy



### Detecting a reported fault is different from predicting it before it starts.



**Decision.** Maintenance analyst reviews persistent unusual regimes. No automated safety action, downtime-reduction or operational maintenance claim.



**Evidence.** At training quantile .995: IsolationForest 30 episodes versus robust baseline 175; 23 IF episodes do not overlap reports. Overlap in 4/4 report windows; prior-24h alerts in 1/4. Valid exposure 142.16 equivalent days.



**Interpretation.** One compressor and four documented windows. No full false-positive ground truth: outside-report episodes are unverified. Threshold grid is a sensitivity, not test tuning. Bins need >=54 observations and are available at closing time; 3-bin persistence. Prior alerts may be unrelated; no causal precursor or maintenance benefit claim.



**Handoff.** Review the source, tests and population boundaries before using this as a business result. This is a portfolio study and local technical demonstration, not a client deployment.
