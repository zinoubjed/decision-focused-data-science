## Notion case-study copy



### The cleaning rule changed the forecasting result more than a more complex model did.



**Decision.** Planning analyst reviews next week’s positive observed sales and uncertainty. No inventory, margin or latent-demand inference.



**Evidence.** Validation selected seasonal_52. On 26 held-out one-week origins: MAE £40,170.66, WAPE 17.40%, signed bias £3,040.39. Separate interval evaluation: 61.54% coverage on 13 weeks. Exact-dedup seasonal WAPE 14.34% is a sensitivity, not a reason to alter the target.



**Interpretation.** Thirteen selection weeks; 26 point-forecast test weeks split into 13 calibration and 13 interval-evaluation weeks. The nominal 90% scenario fails to achieve that coverage; no 90% reliability guarantee. Annual history is short and duplicates ambiguous. Mean-4 is better on this test, but no test-driven model switch is made. Costs are hypothetical.



**Handoff.** Review the source, tests and population boundaries before using this as a business result. This is a portfolio study and local technical demonstration, not a client deployment.
