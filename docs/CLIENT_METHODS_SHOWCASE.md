# Reliable analytics — method presentation for review

These are descriptions of portfolio workflows, not authorised commercial offers, client testimonials or proof of personally verified independent delivery. Before offering any paid service, the user must confirm the service, scope and personal ability to deliver it. No price, timeline, guaranteed saving or ROI is asserted.

## Data preparation and reconciliation

Potential bounded deliverable: a reproducible cleaned table, quality report, key/cardinality checks and reconciliation to original totals. Inputs and exclusions are agreed first. An ambiguous duplicate is flagged or compared in sensitivity scenarios rather than silently deleted. Outputs include a script, data dictionary, exception counts and handoff instructions.

## SQL reporting and operational KPI

Potential bounded deliverable: a query at the agreed analytical grain, documented denominator, maturity rules and reporting table. Many-side tables are aggregated before joining. Small or incomplete cohorts are shown or withheld explicitly. A dashboard can support investigation, but cannot establish a cause or justify sanctions from associations alone.

The Olist study supplies a **method example only** in a commercial presentation. Its data, derived figures, screenshots and numerical outcomes are not included here for commercial reuse. Rights clarification is required before changing that boundary.

## Observed-sales forecasting

Potential bounded deliverable: a horizon-specific historical backtest, simple reference forecasts, error breakdown and uncertainty scenarios. The target must be actual observable sales in the supplied files. Stock optimization requires additional stock, capacity and cost evidence; it is not bundled into a sales forecast by implication.

## Assisted text categorisation

Potential bounded deliverable: a local, access-controlled prototype with category definitions, a dated test set, error analysis and an abstention/review queue. Privacy review and permitted processing precede text use. A high overall score cannot hide weak categories. No complaint or sensitive client text is sent to external models without explicit permission.

## Automation and technical handoff

Potential bounded deliverable: a numeric processing API, input validation, tests, execution guide and a stop/rollback procedure. Local runtime evidence is separated from container, remote CI and cloud evidence. Any public endpoint or cost-bearing service needs separate approval. The current Docker build remains unverified because no engine is available.

The work examples and AI provenance are reviewable in the portfolio. They are not real client projects and are not a claim that every candidate service is already offered.
